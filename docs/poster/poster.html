01 · WHYLegal text should not need a lawyer to read.

The problem

Dense, costly, and generic

Egyptian laws, contracts and rulings are long and written in legal Arabic. A lawyer is expensive, and general chatbots don’t read your document, so they can be confidently wrong about it.

Our solution

Ask your own PDF

Ma‘at reads the PDF you upload, finds the parts that answer your question, and explains them in plain Arabic or English, citing the article numbers.
